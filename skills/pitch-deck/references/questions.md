# Grilling the human

Ask once, after the first version is visible, in one message. Group the questions, number
them, and say in a few words why each matters, so the human can answer in one line each.
Ask only what you could not find out by reading the project.

## Always

- **The thesis in their words.** "If an investor or customer remembers one sentence, which
  is it?" Use their wording for the cover subtitle and the resolution line of the problem
  slide. A framing you invented (for example, "we remove the busywork") can quietly
  contradict theirs (for example, "we give you more ways to grow").
- **The first audience** and the decision you want from them.
- **What may be named:** competitors, customers, partners, logos, quotes.

## Investor

1. Revenue now, growth over the last six months, and the source of each number?
2. Active users and the definition of active?
3. The thesis metric: does the behaviour your product depends on show up in the data (for
   example, users of two features retain better than users of one)?
4. Which job do most users start with? (Decides which problem the deck opens on.)
5. Who is the best customer today, as one concrete type?
6. What is the round: amount, stage, and the milestone it buys?
7. Team: who, and the one fact that makes each person right for this?
8. Is a market or country concentration investors will ask about? Address it head-on.
9. What in the vision is designed, built or only intended?

## Customer

1. Which segment is in the room, and which slide should they see first?
2. The two or three features people adopt right away, in the order they hit them?
3. Can you supply real screenshots from a demo account?
4. Pricing: link to the live page, or a plan to name?
5. Anything built but not yet switched on that people will ask about?

## Partner or enterprise

1. What kind of partner (payments, platform that would embed you, distributor, large
   customer)?
2. Their goal for the partnership, in their words?
3. Team accounts, permissions, an API or integrations: which exist today?
4. Security or compliance questions they have raised?
5. A pilot: scope, success measure, dates, owners?

## After answers

Fill placeholders, remove the slides the answers made irrelevant, and sweep the deck for
every slide that shares a framing the human corrected.
